Multiples of the line bet (the scatter pays the total bet). Wins run left to right on nine fixed lines; the dragon is wild for every symbol.

Free spins

Three gold bags anywhere pay the scatter prize and start 8 free spins. Every win in the round is doubled, and three more bags add 5 to the count. Free spins are held by the vault: log out, come back, and the round is still there.

Rules

  • Nine fixed lines cross the grid, taking one symbol from each of the five reels. A total bet of 90 gold means 10 gold on each of the nine lines.
  • A line pays from the far-left reel only. Three, four or five of the same symbol, in a row, starting on reel 1. The same three symbols starting one reel further right pay nothing.
  • Only symbols on a line count. Three 💎 on three different lines, or in rows that do not line up left to right, are not a win — the rest of the grid is decoration.
  • The dragon 🐉 is wild: it stands in for every symbol except the bag, so 🐉💎💎 also pays as three gems.
  • Each of the nine lines is judged and paid on its own, and the paytable above is a multiple of the line bet, not the total.
  • 💰 is the exception: bags pay anywhere on the grid (2×, 10×, 60× the total bet) and three of them start the free spins.
  • Free spins are played at the bet that won them and cannot be changed mid-round; leaving the page does not lose them.

In short: a payout always starts at the left edge of the grid. Gems that dropped in the middle reels, or that are not on one of the nine lines, pay nothing — that is the machine working as designed, not a lost win.
